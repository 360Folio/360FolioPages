What we offer

Four commitments you can measure us against.

Not a values statement. Four things you can hold us to on the first project, and hold against us on the second.

01

Honest and reliable advice

Every prospective client is advised on the best solution for them, with no bias toward increasing our own margin. If another company fits your needs better, we will say so — and we have.

02

Fast service, multilingual support

Quotes return the same working day, and enquiries reach a producer rather than a queue. We answer in English, Urdu or Arabic — which matters when the crew, the client and the building sit in three different countries.

03

Experience across the whole stack

Not capture alone: design and web solutions too. It is why website integration is included rather than subcontracted, and why the tour and the site it lives on can have a single owner.

Who we are

We take pride in the work and in the relations around it.

The second half of that sentence costs us money occasionally. It is the half we would keep if we could only keep one.

We would rather be useful than booked. All prospective clients are advised on the best solution for them, with no bias toward increasing the profit margin. We are equally happy to point a client toward another company's solution where we think it fits their needs better than ours would.

Our experience in internet solutions means the product incorporates easily into your current website. We can also build the site itself — fully editable by you, with the virtual tour integrated into it — which suits anyone who would rather have one team and one invoice than two of each.

Our creative team custom-tailors each image to the requirement: not only the front end, but the retouching that makes a subject look like itself on its best day.

Our prices are competitive and carry no hidden charges. You will always know exactly what you are paying for, before the shoot rather than after it.

The highest resolution the project needs

We start at 60 megapixels per panorama and run to thousands. That floor is our lowest standard, not our best, and it has never moved downward.

HDR wherever conditions allow

Our photographers shoot High Dynamic Range so that a bright window and a dark corner are both legible in the same frame. It is what makes an interior look like the room you remember.

A prototype before anything is signed off

You criticise a working beta, not a description of one. Design and UX changes go in before final release.

One team for the tour and the site

Capture, post-production, interface and hosting under one roof — so when something breaks there is one number to call, not three.

Every figure we publish describes our own delivered work. Since 2013 · 38 projects in the archive · 3.1 gigapixels at the largest · a 60 MP floor · 3 offices. No third-party market statistics.

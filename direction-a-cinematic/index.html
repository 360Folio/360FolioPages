Why spherical

Places you can walk into from anywhere.

A flat photograph asks people to imagine the rest of the room. Video is closer, but the viewer is still watching someone else's decisions about where to look.

An interactive sphere removes both problems. The visitor decides — they turn towards the window, look up at the ceiling, lean into the detail. That is the difference between seeing a picture of a place and having been there.

Our spheres are complete. Real floor, real ceiling, nothing patched, and no sign of the crew, the tripod or their reflections anywhere in the frame.

Viewer chooses the view

Pan, tilt and zoom under their control — with gyroscope on a phone, so turning the device turns the room.

Whole room in one frame

360° × 180° spherical coverage, floor to ceiling, at a 60 megapixel minimum.

Information where it belongs

Click-to-open hotspots carrying text, images, audio, video, a web page or a PDF — attached to the thing they describe.

Open anywhere

HTML5 in any modern browser, on phones and tablets, and in a headset over WebXR. No plugin, no download.

How work runs

The same seven steps, whichever practice you enter through.

You approve a working prototype before anything is signed off, and nothing is chargeable until you agree the quote at step two.

  1. Assessment

    We visit the facility and work out what the project actually needs.

  2. Concept

    Our approach and idea, with financial and time estimates, in writing.

  3. Production

    On agreement the work begins — shoot, render or build, depending on the practice.

  4. Post-production

    Treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then the interface build.

  5. Beta release

    A working prototype you can click through and criticise.

  6. Your revisions

    Design and UX changes go in before anything is signed off.

  7. Final release

    Integrated into your site, live, and yours.

Not sure which one you need?

Say so. The enquiry form has a "not sure — please advise" option for exactly this. Describe the building and the audience; working out the right format is our job, and the assessment is free.

Formats

Six ways to record a place.

Most projects use two or three together. Whatever the mix, they arrive in one viewer with one set of navigation, branding and access controls.

360° Virtual Tours

Tours let a client experience a location rather than view it. Click a hotspot for the architecture, open the floor plan to see where a room sits in the building. Every tour is custom-built to the client's branding — from full guidelines or just the feel of their current site.

CGI Virtual Tours

Computer-generated tours built from CAD files and a detailed build specification, so you can walk a property before a brick is laid. Rendered in 3ds Max and Lumion. Useful for selling off plan and for the investment conversations that make a project real.

360° Objects

The most truthful way to show a product online. Objects of any size, from jewellery up to cars, are placed on a turntable and photographed in sequence so the viewer can spin them. Hotspots mark the areas of detail worth clicking into.

360° Videos

Live-action VR that places viewers inside the film and lets them look anywhere. Shooting 360° is a different discipline from flat footage — new blocking, new tools, and nowhere for the crew to hide. We have effectively rewritten the rulebook.

Gigapixel Panoramas

Giant photographs stitched from hundreds or thousands of frames. Zoom in far enough and you see detail the naked eye could not have resolved standing there. Our largest is 3,100 megapixels — Islamabad from Daman-e-Koh, Pakistan's first gigapixel panorama.

Large-Format Imaging

Multi-resolution imagery of artwork — vital for restorers and collectors — façades for architects, and any subject needing more detail than one camera holds. Close telephoto work plus mosaic stitching, letting the viewer zoom to within half a degree of field.

The specification

All 33 features, and which ones cost extra.

Published in full, because hidden line items are how this industry usually works. Fifteen are included in the regular price. Eighteen are priced individually, in writing, before you commit.

  • Standard — included (15)
  • Optional — priced separately (18)
01 Highest resolution Our quality · Standard

We start from 60 megapixels as our standard — and perhaps lowest — resolution, running up to thousands of megapixels for gigapixel work.

02 HDR photography Our quality · Standard

High Dynamic Range blends multiple exposures of the same aspect so both bright and dark areas are seen with clarity, making the image as close to reality as possible. We use HDR in all our work.

03 Complete scene Our quality · Standard

A complete panorama including the real floor and ceiling. No Photoshop, no patching, and no sign of our crew or equipment — not their shadows, not their reflections, anywhere in the sphere.

04 Faster dynamic loading Our quality · Standard

Multi-resolution loading of the zone being displayed, so the visible area arrives first and the viewer never waits on pixels outside their field of view.

05 Custom branding with logo integration Experience · Standard

The point of a tour is to show your facilities — your logo inside it, designed to your brand and made linkable, is what reinforces whose facilities they are.

06 Navigation hotspots & controls Experience · Standard

Buttons to pan, tilt and zoom, plus a help screen for first-time viewers. For those who would rather walk than read a map, graphic hotspots inside the panorama move the user from one image to the next with a click.

07 Auto-rotation & auto-tour Experience · Standard

Automatic sequencing of the panoramas, activating when the tour is left untouched for a few seconds — for a screen in a lobby or anywhere nobody is holding the mouse.

08 Full-screen responsive design Experience · Standard

Adapts to every screen size dynamically, from a phone held in one hand to a wall-mounted display.

09 Major browser compatibility — HTML5 Experience · Standard

Built on HTML5 with no plugin dependency of any kind. It opens in the browser your visitors already have.

10 Mobile & tablet compatibility Experience · Standard

Full compatibility with mobile and tablet devices — not a reduced version, the same tour.

11 Gyroscope control Experience · Standard

View the tour by turning the device itself. Turn the phone and the view turns with it — the closest thing to standing in the room without a headset.

12 VR headset mode Experience · Standard

Compatible with head-mounted displays over WebXR — Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro and HTC Vive among them. The same URL that opens on a laptop opens in the headset.

13 Social media integration with direct-linking Experience · Standard

Visitors share the tour in one click. Direct-linking carries the exact view, so whoever opens the link lands where the sharer was standing and looking.

14 Google Analytics integration Experience · Standard

Measure conversions and see how visitors find and move through the tour — which scenes hold them and which they skip.

15 Multiple language support Experience · Optional

Interface, labels and hotspot content in more than one language, switchable inside the tour. We work in English, Urdu and Arabic.

16 Website or PDF embedding Experience · Optional

Your website or any custom HTML page opens in a popup inside the tour. A PDF document or catalogue can be embedded the same way, so the brochure lives where the room is.

17 Domain restriction Security · Optional

The tour runs only on domains you nominate. Nobody else can lift it onto their own website, however they obtained the files.

18 Time restriction Security · Optional

The tour expires on a set date and will not run after it — useful for a campaign, a tender window or a licensed exhibition.

19 Offline restriction Security · Optional

The tour will not run from a local copy. Saving the files to a computer or phone produces nothing that works.

20 Dynamic video — a virtual guide Multimedia · Optional

Partial spherical video embedded into a still panorama to show movement — a process running, or a person speaking to camera as a guide who appears to be standing in the room with the viewer.

21 Standard video embed Multimedia · Optional

Rather than leaving a television or LED screen in shot blank, a video plays on it inside the tour.

22 Photo galleries or images Multimedia · Optional

Galleries or individual images added inside the scene, each with its own title and caption.

23 Still images or object photography Multimedia · Optional

We photograph the items, objects or scenes you want embedded as flat images within a panorama — useful where a detail deserves more resolution than the sphere gives it.

24 Click-to-open media hotspots Multimedia · Optional

Hotspots that open any media on click — text, an image, a sound, a video, a website, an HTML page or a PDF document.

25 Background music or sound Multimedia · Optional

A sound, voice-over or piece of music set as the background of one panorama or of the whole tour.

26 Audio recording or computerised voice-over Multimedia · Optional

We can hire a voice artist to record narration. A computerised voice-over facility is also available in male and female voices, English only.

27 Multi-level floor plan integration Wayfinding · Optional

Interactive 2D or 3D floor plans with clickable spots linking to each location. Buildings with several levels render as multiple plans. If you do not have plans, we can have them designed in either format.

28 Dynamic map integration Wayfinding · Optional

OpenStreetMap, Bing Maps, Google Maps or Yandex embedded and linked to the scenes, so a viewer can see where in the world each panorama sits.

29 GPS-based positioning Wayfinding · Optional

Each panorama carries its real coordinates, which places it precisely on any integrated map.

30 Compass and radar Wayfinding · Optional

The compass shows which way the viewer is facing; the radar shows their current field of view on the plan. Together they stop people getting lost in large buildings.

31 Little-planet introduction Effects · Optional

The tour opens as a tiny planet that unfolds over a few seconds into the normal view. A strong first impression, and it teaches the viewer without words that they are inside a sphere.

32 Rain & snow effects Effects · Optional

Weather overlaid on an outdoor scene — used sparingly, for seasonal campaigns or to show a location in conditions the shoot day did not provide.

33 Panoramic projections menu Effects · Standard

A contextual menu listing every available projection: normal, fisheye, architectural, stereographic, pannini, little planet and fisheye ball. All our projects include this menu, reached by right-clicking inside the tour.

Free as standard: non-binding quotes, logo integration, HDR photography and website integration. The optional eighteen are quoted individually so you can see exactly what each one adds.

Process

You approve a prototype before anything is signed off.

Seven steps from first call to launch. Quality does not have to be expensive — our basic package carries extras other providers itemise.

  1. Assessment

    We visit the facility and work out what the project actually needs.

  2. Concept

    Our approach and idea, with financial and time estimates, in writing.

  3. Photography

    On agreement the shoot begins — HDR, complete spheres, no crew visible.

  4. Post-production

    RAW treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then the interface build.

  5. Beta release

    A working prototype you can click through and criticise.

  6. Your revisions

    Design and UX changes go in before anything is signed off.

  7. Final release

    Integrated into your site, live, and yours.

02

We take pride in the work and in the relations around it.

The second half of that sentence costs us money occasionally. It is the half we would keep if we could only keep one.

02.1

We would rather be useful than booked.

All prospective clients are advised on the best solution for them, with no bias toward increasing the profit margin. We are equally happy to point a client toward another company's solution, where we think it fits their needs better than ours would.

That has cost us work. It has also brought a good deal back.

02.2

One team for the tour and the site.

Our experience in internet solutions means the product incorporates easily into your current website. We can also build the site itself — fully editable by you, with the virtual tour integrated into it.

That suits anyone who would rather have one team and one invoice than two of each, and one number to call when something breaks.

02.3

Each image is tailored, not processed.

Our creative team custom-tailors every image to the requirement: not only the front end, but the retouching that makes a subject look like itself on its best day.

We produce at the highest resolution the project needs, and wherever conditions allow our photographers shoot HDR, so a bright window and a dark corner are both legible in one frame.

On price — our prices are competitive and carry no hidden charges. You will always know exactly what you are paying for, before the shoot rather than after it. Request a fixed quote →

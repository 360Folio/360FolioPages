01

Six ways to record a place.

Most projects combine two or three. Whatever the mix, they arrive in one viewer with one set of navigation, branding and access controls.

01.1

360° Virtual Tours

A tour lets a visitor do more than look at your location — it lets them move through it. Curious about the architecture? Click a hotspot. Want to know where this room sits in the building? Open the floor plan.

Every tour is custom-built for the client, matched to their branding and to the site it will live in. We can work from full brand guidelines or simply from the look and feel of your current pages.

01.2

CGI Virtual Tours

We are at the forefront of computer-generated imagery and of 360° tours, so we combined the two. The result lets you visualise and experience a property before a single brick has been laid.

We work from CAD files and a detailed build specification, so the finished tour looks and feels as true to life as the completed building. Rendered in Autodesk 3ds Max and Lumion.

01.3

360° Objects

Object photography is the most truthful way to show a product online. Objects of any size — from jewellery up to cars — are placed on a turntable and photographed in sequence, so the viewer can spin them.

Hotspots are captured too, letting users click into particular areas of detail.

01.4

360° Videos

Also known as VR video or live-action VR, this places viewers inside the world of a film and lets them look anywhere in it.

Shooting 360° differs from traditional filming in almost every respect. As we have developed we have nearly rewritten the rulebook, building a set of tools and techniques specific to the format — which is why unusual shoots do not frighten us.

01.5

Gigapixel Panoramas

Giant photographs made from hundreds or thousands of individual images stitched together. The resolution can be so great that you zoom in and see detail you would not have been able to see with the human eye.

Our largest is a 3,100 megapixel view of Islamabad from Daman-e-Koh in the Margalla Hills — Pakistan's first gigapixel panorama.

01.6

Large-Format Imaging

Multi-resolution imagery of almost anything — works of art, which is vital for restorers and collectors, façades for architects, or any subject needing detail beyond what the best single camera can hold.

Achieved through close telephoto work combined with mosaic stitching, letting the viewer zoom to within half a degree of field.

02

All thirty-three features, and what each one costs.

Published in full, because hidden line items are how this industry usually works. Fifteen are included in the regular price; eighteen are quoted individually, in writing, before you commit.

  • Standard — included (15)
  • Optional — priced separately (18)
Highest resolution

We start from 60 megapixels as our standard — and perhaps lowest — resolution, up to thousands of megapixels for gigapixel work.

HDR photography

A blend of multiple exposures of the same aspect so both bright and dark areas are seen with clarity. We use HDR in all our work.

Complete scene

Real floor and real ceiling. No patching, and no sign of the crew or their equipment — not their shadows, not their reflections.

Faster dynamic loading

Multi-resolution loading of the zone being displayed, so the visible area arrives first.

Custom branding with logo integration

Your logo inside the tour, designed to your brand and made linkable — useful for reinforcing whose facilities these are.

Navigation hotspots & controls

Buttons to pan, tilt and zoom with a help screen for new viewers, plus graphic hotspots to walk from one image to the next.

Auto-rotation & auto-tour

Automatic sequencing that activates when the tour is left untouched — for a screen in a lobby or a public place.

Full-screen responsive design

Adapts to every screen size dynamically, from a phone to a wall-mounted display.

Major browser compatibility — HTML5

No plugin dependency of any kind. It opens in the browser your visitors already have.

Mobile & tablet compatibility

Not a reduced version — the same tour, on every device.

Gyroscope control

View the tour by turning the device. Turn the phone and the view turns with it.

VR headset mode

Compatible with head-mounted displays over WebXR — Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro and HTC Vive among them.

Social media integration with direct-linking

Share in one click. Direct-linking carries the exact view, so the next viewer lands where the sharer was looking.

Google Analytics integration

Measure conversions and see how visitors find and move through the tour.

Multiple language support

Interface, labels and hotspot content in more than one language. We work in English, Urdu and Arabic.

Website or PDF embedding

Your website or any custom HTML page opens in a popup inside the tour; a PDF or catalogue can be embedded the same way.

Domain restriction

The tour runs only on domains you nominate, so nobody else can host it on their own website.

Time restriction

The tour expires on a set date and will not run after it — for a campaign, a tender window or a licensed exhibition.

Offline restriction

The tour will not run from a local copy. Saving the files produces nothing that works.

Dynamic video — a virtual guide

Partial spherical video embedded in a still panorama: a process running, or a person speaking as a guide standing in the room.

Standard video embed

Rather than leaving a television or LED screen blank in shot, a video plays on it inside the tour.

Photo galleries or images

Galleries or individual images added inside the scene, each with a title and caption.

Still images or object photography

We photograph the items or scenes you want embedded as flat images inside a panorama.

Click-to-open media hotspots

Hotspots that open any media on click — text, image, sound, video, a website, an HTML page or a PDF.

Background music or sound

A sound, voice-over or piece of music set as the background of one panorama or of the whole tour.

Audio recording or computerised voice-over

A hired voice artist, or a computerised voice-over in male and female voices, English only.

Multi-level floor plan integration

Interactive 2D or 3D plans with clickable spots linking to each location. If you have none, we can have them designed.

Dynamic map integration

OpenStreetMap, Bing Maps, Google Maps or Yandex, linked to the scenes.

GPS-based positioning

Each panorama carries its real coordinates, placing it precisely on any integrated map.

Compass and radar

The compass shows the viewing direction; the radar shows the current field of view.

Little-planet introduction

The tour opens as a tiny planet that unfolds into the standard view — a strong first impression, and it teaches the viewer they are inside a sphere.

Rain & snow effects

Weather overlaid on an outdoor scene, for seasonal campaigns or conditions the shoot day did not provide.

Panoramic projections menu

A contextual menu of every projection: normal, fisheye, architectural, stereographic, pannini, little planet and fisheye ball. All our projects include it.

04

You approve a prototype first.

Seven steps from first call to launch. Nothing is chargeable until you agree the quote at step two.

  • 01Assessment — we visit the facility and work out what the project actually needs.
  • 02Concept — our approach and idea in writing, with financial and time estimates.
  • 03Photography — on agreement the shoot begins: HDR, complete spheres, no crew visible.
  • 04Post-production — RAW treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then the interface.
  • 05Beta release — a working prototype you can click through and criticise.
  • 06Your revisions — design and UX changes go in before anything is signed off.
  • 07Final release — integrated into your site, live, and yours.

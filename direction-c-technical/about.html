§ 01 — What we offer

Four things, and we will hold you to them

Not a values statement. Four commitments you can measure us against on the first project, and hold against us on the second.

Offer 01

Honest and reliable advice

Every prospective client is advised on the best solution for them, with no bias toward increasing our own margin. If we think another company suits your needs better, we will say so — that is a real sentence from our old site and it still holds.

Offer 02

Fast service, multilingual support

Quotes come back the same business day. Enquiries reach a producer rather than a queue, and we answer in English, Urdu or Arabic — which matters when the crew, the client and the building are in three different countries.

Offer 03

Experience across the whole stack

Not capture alone: design and web solutions too. It is why website integration is included rather than subcontracted, and why the tour and the site it lives on can have a single owner.

Offer 04NO EXTRAS

Competitive prices, nothing hidden

Quotes are fixed and itemised, so you always know exactly what you are paying for. Fifteen of the thirty-three features are included in the regular price; the other eighteen are priced individually, in writing, before you commit.

§ 02 — How we work

We take pride in the work and in the relations around it

The second half of that sentence is the one that costs us money occasionally, and it is the one we would keep if we could only keep one.

We would rather be useful than booked. All prospective clients are advised on the best solution for them, with no bias toward increasing the profit margin. We are equally happy to point a client at another company's solution, if we think it fits their needs better than ours would.

Our experience in internet solutions means the product incorporates easily into your current website. We can also build the site itself — fully editable by you, with the virtual tour integrated into it — which suits anyone who would rather have one team and one invoice than two of each.

What the creative team does

Each image is custom-tailored to the requirement, not only in the front end but in the retouching that makes the subject look like itself on its best day. We produce images at the highest resolution the project needs, and wherever conditions allow, our photographers shoot HDR so that bright windows and dark corners are both legible in the same frame.

Our prices are competitive and carry no hidden charges. You will always know exactly what you are paying for, before the shoot rather than after it.

What that means in practice

  • Free, non-binding quotes — usually returned the same business day.
  • A prototype before sign-off — you criticise a working beta, not a description.
  • HDR on every frame — the exposure work is not an upgrade.
  • Complete spheres — real floor, real ceiling, no crew in the reflections.
  • Website integration included — we are the web team as well.

§ 03 — Before you write

What makes a quote exact instead of approximate

None of this is required. Every item you can answer moves the first number closer to the final one, and shortens the assessment.

01

The location, precisely

City and country at minimum. Travel is the one cost we cannot estimate without it, and it is the single biggest variable between a quote and a final invoice.

02

How many spaces

A rough count of rooms, floors or viewpoints you want people to be able to stand in. Round numbers are fine — six or so, about twenty, the whole campus.

03

Which optional features

Floor plans, embedded video, narration, background audio, object photography, multiple languages. Eighteen options are priced individually and listed in full.

See the feature matrix →

04

Where it will live

Your own site, a kiosk, a headset at a trade show, or all three. Website integration is included, so tell us the platform and we will handle the embed.

05

Any fixed date

An exhibition, an intake deadline, an opening. Capture is the short part; post-production and revisions are what need the runway.

06

Access constraints

Restricted areas, security clearance, times the building is empty. We shoot around operations, but knowing the window in advance keeps the crew count down.

§ 05 — Questions

The six things people ask before enquiring

If your question is not here, put it in the message field — it gets answered in the reply, not in a follow-up call.

01What does a virtual tour actually cost?

There is no list price, because the two things that drive the number — how many spaces and how far the crew travels — change with every building. What we can promise is the shape of the answer: fixed, itemised, and delivered the same business day.

Our basic package already includes several things other studios quote separately: HDR photography, logo integration and custom branding, and website integration. The eighteen optional features are priced individually so you can see exactly what each one adds.

02Do we have to close the building while you shoot?

No. We work around normal operations, and we have shot working hospitals, universities and government facilities without shutting them.

The finished sphere shows no sign of the crew or the equipment — not the tripod, not a shadow, not a reflection in the glass. That is a capture and post-production discipline, not retouching: the floor and ceiling in your tour are the real floor and ceiling, not patched.

03How long does the whole thing take?

Photography is usually the shortest phase. The runway is in post-production — RAW treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then the interactive front end — followed by a beta release you click through and criticise before anything is signed off.

Give us your fixed date in the enquiry and the concept stage will tell you honestly whether it is achievable.

04Will it work on phones and headsets, or only desktops?

All three. Everything is HTML5 with no plugin dependency, responsive to any screen size, and fully compatible with mobile and tablet devices including gyroscope control — turn the phone and the view turns with it.

VR mode is supported for head-mounted displays over WebXR, and the same tour can be projected in a lobby with auto-rotation and auto-tour running unattended.

05Can it live on our own website, under our own branding?

Yes, and integration into your existing site is included rather than extra — we can also build the site itself if you would rather have one team. Your logo sits inside the viewer and can be made linkable.

If you need the tour locked down, we can restrict it to specific domains so it cannot be re-hosted elsewhere, set an expiry date, or prevent it running from a local copy.

06Do you travel outside Pakistan and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Crews travel from whichever of the three offices is closest, which is why the location field matters — it is the difference between a range and an exact number.

Work to date spans Pakistan, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the United States, for clients including national heritage bodies, hospitals, universities and government authorities.

§ 03 — Why spherical

A flat photograph asks people to imagine the rest

Video is closer, but the viewer still watches someone else's choices. Only an interactive sphere lets them decide where to look.

§ 05 — Delivery

Seven steps from first call to launch

You see a working prototype before you approve anything. Quotes are fixed and itemised — quality does not have to be expensive, and a good tour pays for itself well before you refurbish.

  1. Step 01

    Assessment

    We visit the facility and work out what the project actually needs.

  2. Step 02

    Concept

    Our approach, plus the financial and time estimates, in writing.

  3. Step 03

    Photography

    On agreement, the shoot begins — HDR, complete spheres, no crew visible.

  4. Step 04

    Post-production

    RAW treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then UX build.

  5. Step 05

    Beta release

    A working prototype you can click through and criticise.

  6. Step 06

    Your revisions

    Design and UX changes go in before anything is signed off.

  7. Step 07

    Final release

    Integrated into your site, live, and yours.

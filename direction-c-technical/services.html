§ 04 — How work runs

The same seven steps, whichever practice you enter through

You see a working prototype before you approve anything. Nothing is chargeable until you agree the quote at step two.

  1. Step 01

    Assessment

    We visit the facility and work out what the project actually needs.

  2. Step 02

    Concept

    Our approach, plus the financial and time estimates, in writing.

  3. Step 03

    Production

    On agreement the work begins — shoot, render or build, depending on the practice.

  4. Step 04

    Post-production

    Treatment, retouching, stitching and projection, then the interface build.

  5. Step 05

    Beta release

    A working prototype you can click through and criticise.

  6. Step 06

    Your revisions

    Design and UX changes go in before anything is signed off.

  7. Step 07

    Final release

    Integrated into your site, live, and yours.

What you get from us

  • Honest and reliable advice — including when another company suits you better.
  • Fast service and multilingual support — English, Urdu and Arabic.
  • Experience across design and web solutions, not capture alone.
  • Competitive prices with no hidden extras — what you get is more than you pay for.

Where to go next

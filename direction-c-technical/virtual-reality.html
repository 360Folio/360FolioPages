§ 01 — Formats

Six ways to record a place

Most projects combine two or three of these. Whatever the mix, they arrive in one viewer with one set of navigation, branding and access controls.

Format 01MOST ORDERED

360° Virtual Tours

Virtual tours let a client do more than view your location — they let them experience it. Want to know more about the architecture? Click a hotspot. Want to see where this room sits in the building? Open the floor plan.

Every tour is custom-built to fit the client's branding and website as a whole. We can work from full brand guidelines or simply from the current look and feel of your pages.

Coverage
360° × 180° spherical
Navigation
Hotspots, floor plans, maps
Typical use
Campuses, hospitals, hotels

Format 02BEFORE IT EXISTS

CGI Virtual Tours

We are at the forefront of both computer-generated imagery and 360° tours, so we combined them. You can visualise and experience a property before a single brick has been laid.

We work from CAD files and a detailed build specification, so the finished tour looks and feels as true to life as the completed building. Useful for selling off plan, and for the capital conversations that make a project real.

Input
CAD files + build spec
Rendered in
Autodesk 3ds Max · Lumion
Typical use
Off-plan sales, investment

Format 03

360° Objects

Object photography is the most truthful way to show a product online. Anything from jewellery up to cars is placed on a turntable and photographed in sequence, so the viewer can spin it themselves.

Hotspots can be captured too, letting users click into particular areas of detail.

Subject size
Jewellery → vehicles
Method
Sequenced turntable
Delivered
Ceramic Kaftan · Preserved Hand

Format 04

360° Videos

Also called VR video or live-action VR. It places viewers inside the world of a film and lets them look anywhere in it, which is a different discipline from shooting flat footage.

As 360 filmmakers we have effectively rewritten the rulebook — new tools, new blocking, nowhere for the crew to hide. That iterative process is why we can solve unusual shoots quickly.

Also called
VR video, live-action VR
Best for
Events, process, training
Delivered
Lok Mela · Saudi Post events

Format 053.1 GP RECORD

Gigapixel Panoramas

Giant photographs built from hundreds or thousands of individual images stitched together. The resolution can be so great that you zoom in and see detail you could not have resolved with the naked eye standing in the same spot.

Our largest is a 3,100 megapixel view of Islamabad from Daman-e-Koh in the Margalla Hills — Pakistan's first gigapixel panorama.

Multi-resolution imagery of almost anything — works of art, which is vital for restorers and collectors, façades for architects, or any subject needing detail beyond what a single camera can hold.

Achieved through close telephoto work combined with mosaic stitching, letting the viewer zoom to within half a degree of field.

§ 03 — Quality floor

The four things we will not compromise

These are not upgrades and they are not negotiable downwards. They are the baseline on every project we take, which is why the word "standard" means something in the matrix below.

Floor 0160 MP MINIMUM

Highest resolution

We start at 60 megapixels as our standard — and that is our lowest, not our best. Gigapixel work runs into thousands of megapixels. Resolution is what lets a viewer lean in rather than squint.

Floor 02EVERY FRAME

HDR photography

High Dynamic Range blends multiple exposures of the same aspect so bright windows and dark corners are both legible. It is what makes an interior look like the room you remember rather than a photograph of it. We use HDR in all our work.

Floor 03NOTHING PATCHED

Complete scene

A complete sphere including the real floor and the real ceiling. No Photoshop, no patching, and no sign of our crew or equipment anywhere — not the tripod, not a shadow, not a reflection in the glass.

Floor 04PROGRESSIVE

Faster dynamic loading

Multi-resolution tiling means the zone being viewed loads first and sharpens as it arrives. A 3-gigapixel panorama opens as quickly as a small one, because the viewer never waits for pixels they cannot see.

Our quality

High Dynamic Range photography blends multiple exposures of the same aspect so both bright and dark areas are seen with clarity. It makes images appear as close to reality as possible. We use HDR in all our work.

User experience

12 items
05Custom branding with logo integrationStandard

The point of a tour is to show your facilities — but your logo appearing inside it, designed to your brand and made linkable, is what reinforces whose facilities they are.

06Navigation hotspots & controlsStandard

Buttons to pan, tilt and zoom, plus a help screen for first-time viewers. For those who would rather walk than use a map, graphic hotspots inside the panorama move the user from one image to the next with a click.

07Auto-rotation & auto-tourStandard

Automatic sequencing of the panoramas, activating when the tour is left untouched for a few seconds. Useful on a screen in a lobby or any public place where nobody is holding the mouse.

08Full-screen responsive designStandard

Adapts to every screen size dynamically, from a phone held in one hand to a wall-mounted display.

09Major browser compatibility — HTML5Standard

Built on HTML5 with no plugin dependency of any kind. It opens in the browser your visitors already have.

10Mobile & tablet compatibilityStandard

Full compatibility with mobile and tablet devices — not a reduced version, the same tour.

11Gyroscope controlStandard

View the tour by turning the device itself. Turn the phone and the view turns with it, which is the closest thing to standing in the room without a headset.

12VR headset modeStandard

Compatible with head-mounted displays over WebXR — Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro and HTC Vive among them. The same URL that opens on a laptop opens in the headset.

13Social media integration with direct-linkingStandard

Visitors share the tour to any network in one click. Direct-linking carries the exact view with it, so whoever opens the link lands where the sharer was standing and looking.

14Google Analytics integrationStandard

Measure conversions and see how visitors are finding and moving through the tour — which scenes hold them and which they skip.

15Multiple language supportOptional

The full interface, labels and hotspot content in more than one language, switchable inside the tour. We work in English, Urdu and Arabic.

16Website or PDF embeddingOptional

Your website or any custom HTML page can open in a popup inside the tour. A PDF document or catalogue can be embedded the same way, so the brochure lives where the room is.

3 items

The tour runs only on domains you nominate. Nobody else can lift it onto their own website, however they obtained the files.

18Time restrictionOptional

The tour expires on a set date and will not run after it — useful for a campaign, a tender window or a licensed exhibition.

19Offline restrictionOptional

The tour will not run from a local copy. Saving the files to a computer or phone produces nothing that works.

22Photo galleries or imagesOptional

Galleries or individual images added inside the scene, each with its own title and caption.

23Still images or object photographyOptional

We photograph the items, objects or scenes you want embedded as flat images within a panorama — useful where a detail deserves more resolution than the sphere gives it.

24Click-to-open media hotspotsOptional

Hotspots that open any media on click — a text description, an image, a sound, a video, a website, an HTML page or a PDF document.

25Background music or soundOptional

A sound, voice-over or piece of music set as the background of one panorama or of the whole tour.

26Audio recording or computerised voice-overOptional

We can hire a voice artist to record narration for the tour. A computerised voice-over facility is also available in male and female voices, English only.

Maps & floor plans

4 items
27Multi-level floor plan integrationOptional

Interactive 2D or 3D floor plans of your building, with clickable spots linking to each location. Where a building has several levels these render as multiple plans, giving a fast way to move through the tour. If you do not have plans, we can have them designed in either format.

28Dynamic map integrationOptional

OpenStreetMap, Bing Maps, Google Maps or Yandex embedded and linked to the scenes, so a viewer can see where in the world each panorama sits.

29GPS-based positioningOptional

Each panorama carries its real coordinates, which places it precisely on any integrated map.

30Compass and radarOptional

The compass shows which way the viewer is facing; the radar shows the current field of view on the plan. Together they stop people getting lost in large buildings.

Special effects

3 items
31Little-planet introductionOptional

The tour opens as a tiny planet that unfolds over a few seconds into the normal view. It is a strong first impression, and it teaches the viewer without words that they are inside a sphere.

32Rain & snow effectsOptional

Weather overlaid on an outdoor scene — used sparingly, for seasonal campaigns or to show a location in conditions the shoot day did not provide.

33Panoramic projections menuStandard

A contextual menu listing every available projection: normal, fisheye, architectural, stereographic, pannini, little planet and fisheye ball. All our projects include this menu, reached by right-clicking inside the tour.

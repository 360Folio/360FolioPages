360Folio — three design directions

Same content, same brief, three interpretations. Each now has six pages: Home, the Services hub, the Virtual Reality page (the densest content problem on the site), the Work archive, About and Contact. Every page carries one marked animation section whose scene is not yet specified.

All three now switch between light and dark — six looks to choose from, not three. The control sits in the header of every page (A calls it Night/Day, B calls it Night edition/Day edition, C calls it Dark/Light). Each remembers your choice and follows your system setting until you override it.

Things to try while comparing. Flip each one between light and dark before judging it — several of these read very differently in the other edition, which is the point of having the switch. Left alone, A and C open dark and B opens light, and all three follow your system setting until you press the control. On the Virtual Reality pages, C’s feature matrix has filter and expand-all controls; that section is the direct comparison point between the three. Resize to phone width — all three hold without horizontal scroll.

The Work pages carry the real archive — all 38 delivered projects with their actual clients, locations, formats and dates, pulled from the old site rather than mocked up. C’s version filters by capture format; A and B present the archive whole.

The animation sections are marked placeholders, not built scenes. Each reserves its final height so the real scene drops in without shifting the layout. Their specs are the next conversation — there are now five per direction.
